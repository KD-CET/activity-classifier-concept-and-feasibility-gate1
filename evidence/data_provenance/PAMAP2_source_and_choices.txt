Training data: PAMAP2 Physical Activity Monitoring (public dataset)
Reference: A. Reiss and D. Stricker, "Introducing a New Benchmarked Dataset for Activity
Monitoring," ISWC 2012. Dataset documents: see the UCI repository page (data/README.md).

Why a public dataset
  The project supervisor allowed outside datasets on the condition that the sensor placement and the
  data collected match the inference system. The watch is worn on the wrist, so only PAMAP2's
  wrist sensor is used.

What was used
  Sensor: the IMU over the wrist of the dominant arm (subject 108 left wrist, others right).
          The +-16 g accelerometer (recommended by the dataset readme; the +-6 g one saturates
          during running) and the gyroscope. Chest and ankle sensors are NOT used.
  Subjects: all 9 (101-109). Subject 109 contributes sedentary data only.
  Sessions: protocol and optional.
  Classes:
    sedentary = lying, sitting, standing, watching TV, computer work, car driving
    walking   = walking
    running   = running
  Test only (never trained on): ascending/descending stairs, ironing, folding laundry,
    vacuum cleaning, house cleaning.
  Tried and rejected (Nov 11): ironing and folding laundry as sedentary. It made vacuuming,
    house cleaning and going downstairs look mostly sedentary and raised the error to 5.1 %
    (see C2_before_after_sedentary_versions.csv for the kept versions).
  Not used: cycling, Nordic walking (poles), playing soccer, rope jumping, transient (id 0).
  Rows where the wrist sensor dropped out (NaN) are removed.

Conversion to match the watch (done in the notebook)
  m/s^2 -> g, rad/s -> deg/s, 100 Hz -> 40 Hz (anti-alias resampling), 1.5 s windows.

Reproducing the data
  build_wrist.py turns the raw PAMAP2 .dat files into the CSVs in data.
  dataset_summary_minutes.csv and dataset_minutes_by_activity.csv list what went in.

Limitations
  PAMAP2's sensor (Colibri IMU) is not the watch's sensor (QMI8658). Wrist placement matches,
  but the same motion can read slightly differently, so the error on the watch must be checked
  with recordings from the watch itself.
