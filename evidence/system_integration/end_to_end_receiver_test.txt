End-to-end test of the dashboard back end (PC only, no watch)
Date: November 11, 2026

Setup
  receiver.py running on port 8765 with a fresh SQLite database.
  simulator.py acting as the watch:
    --change-every 2 --heartbeat 1 --changes 6 --end
    --loss 0.25     (25 % of sends never arrive)
    --ackloss 0.2   (20 % arrive but the acknowledgement is lost, so the "watch" resends)

Result
  Events stored: 12 (session_start, 6 activity changes, heartbeats, session_end)
  Duplicate resends rejected by the receiver: 6   (all ACKed, none stored twice)
  Every stored seq number unique: yes
  core/metrics.compute(): 1 session, closed by session_end; minutes split across
  sedentary / walking / running; no double counting from the duplicates.

What this shows
  The receiver side of "no report lost or counted twice" works under simulated loss.
What it does NOT show
  It does not test the watch's radio or the watch's resend code (WatchReporter), so it is
  not evidence for C3-C5 on its own. Those need the watch sending over a degraded link.
