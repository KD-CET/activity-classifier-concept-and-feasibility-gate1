Watch link code tested on a PC (simulated Wi-Fi and broker)
Date: November 13, 2026        Code: firmware/ActivityClassifier/activity_link.h

Method
  The watch's real link task (outbox, resend until ack, Wi-Fi/clock/server states) was compiled
  on a PC with stand-ins for Wi-Fi and the MQTT library. The stand-in broker dropped 20 % of
  reports and 20 % of acks, and rejected duplicates like the real receiver (db.py).
  Script: a session start, an activity change every 20 s for 5 minutes, a session end, and a
  60 s Wi-Fi outage from 100 s to 160 s.

Result
  All 14 activity changes + session_start + session_end stored exactly once, in order
  (sequence numbers strictly increasing). Reports made during the outage (105, 125, 145 s)
  were sent after it with their real time stamps. No heartbeats queued during the outage.
  Every message passed the real receiver's validation (receiver.validate).

Limits
  Simulated network, not the real radio. The real-link tests (C3-C5) are still to be done.
