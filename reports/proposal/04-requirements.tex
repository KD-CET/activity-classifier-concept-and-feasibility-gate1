\section{Provisional Requirements}
\label{sec:reqs}

All requirements below are provisional: none has been tested yet,
and each is marked so. The healthy-link, degraded-link and outage
conditions referred to in Table~\ref{tab:reqs} are defined in
Section~\ref{sec:poc}.

\begin{table*}[t]
\centering
\footnotesize

\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Class} & \textbf{Statement} & \textbf{Rationale} & \textbf{Method: acceptance criterion} & \textbf{Need} \\
\midrule

REQ-AC-01 & Functional &
The system shall classify the wearer's activity as sedentary,
walking, or running from wrist, chest and/or thigh sensor data. &
Directly answers N1: automatic tracking with no manual logging
from the wearer. &
Demonstration: correct label shown live during a scripted
sit/walk/run session. &
N1 \\[0.6em]

REQ-AC-02 & Performance &
The classifier shall achieve a mean classification error of at
most 15\% across left-out wearers. &
Keeps daily active-minute totals usable; an achievable target
given a five-volunteer dataset. &
Test: leave-one-wearer-out evaluation across all five
volunteers, error averaged. &
N1 \\[0.6em]

REQ-AC-03 & Performance &
The system shall report each detected activity change to the
companion computer within 10\,s when the wireless link is
healthy. &
Fast enough to feel current for habit-forming feedback (N2);
reporting only on change, rather than streaming, keeps network
traffic light -- resolves the stakeholder collision in
Section~\ref{sec:scope}. &
Test: measured delivery latency across repeated activity
changes during a 30-minute mixed-activity session, link healthy. &
N2 \\[0.6em]

REQ-AC-04 & Performance &
The system shall report each detected activity change to the
companion computer within 30\,s when the wireless link is
degraded. &
Allows room for retries on a degraded link while still keeping
feedback usefully timely. &
Test: delivery latency measured under the degraded-link profile
in Section~\ref{sec:poc}. &
N2 \\[0.6em]

REQ-AC-05 & Functional &
The system shall buffer activity reports during a full link
outage and retry until every buffered report is delivered once
the link recovers. &
A silent gap in feedback during an outage would work against
the habit-forming purpose of N2. &
Test: link cut for the outage duration in
Section~\ref{sec:poc}, then restored; computer log checked for
completeness. &
N2 \\[0.6em]

REQ-AC-06 & Constraint &
The trained on-device classifier shall fit within the watch's
available memory budget (C1). &
C1 (Section~\ref{sec:platform}) bounds model size; verifying this
before full training avoids discovering an oversized model late. &
Inspection: compiled model size checked against the memory
available on the watch (C1). &
N1 \\[0.6em]

REQ-AC-07 & Interface &
The custom board shall transmit its feature packets to the
watch over a wireless link. &
Matches the architecture fixed in Section~\ref{sec:platform};
keeps the watch's limited expansion pads free. &
Demonstration: board-to-watch data observed arriving over the
wireless link during a live session, no wired connection
present. &
N1 \\[0.6em]

REQ-AC-08 & Buildability &
The project's firmware and training pipeline shall deploy to a
clean watch and board from one documented command. &
A collaborator, or the author after a failure, must be able to
rebuild the system without undocumented manual steps. &
Demonstration: deploy succeeds on a second computer with no
manual edits to source. &
N3 \\[0.6em]

REQ-AC-09 & Performance &
Fused classification error shall be measured and reported
alongside wrist-only error on the same leave-one-wearer-out
evaluation. &
Shows whether the custom board earns its place; addresses R3
(Section~\ref{sec:poc}). &
Test: per-wearer error compared for wrist-only, board-only and
full-fusion configurations. &
N1 \\[0.6em]

REQ-AC-10 & Functional &
When a board feature stream is missing, the system shall classify
on the remaining features, down to wrist alone, and tag each
report with the sensors that produced it. &
Keeps feedback continuous through a sensor dropout (N2) without
presenting a fallback result as an ordinary one. &
Demonstration: board sensors disconnected mid-session; reports
continue and carry the correct tag. &
N2 \\[0.6em]

REQ-AC-11 & Performance &
The system shall report the session's accumulated active minutes,
with the reported activity agreeing with a labelled reference
recording on at least 85\% of 1-minute intervals. &
Carries the Objective (Section~\ref{sec:need}) into a testable
requirement. &
Test: 30-minute scripted mixed-activity session compared
interval by interval against the reference labels. &
N1 \\[0.6em]

REQ-AC-12 & Functional &
The companion computer shall show the wearer's active minutes for
the current session alongside their previous sessions. &
N2 asks for progress and comparison with past days, not a raw
count alone. &
Demonstration: progress view shown after at least three logged
sessions. &
N2 \\
