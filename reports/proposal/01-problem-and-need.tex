\section{Problem and User Need}
\label{sec:need}

Many adults do not get enough regular physical activity to lower
their risk of obesity-related conditions such as cardiovascular
disease and type II diabetes, and they have no simple way to see
how much they actually move over the course of a day.

\textbf{N1:} ``I don't really know how active I am in my everyday
life -- I want something that tracks my regular (lifestyle)
activity without me having to log anything myself.''

\textbf{N2:} ``I want more than a raw activity count -- a number
alone doesn't help me stick with it. I want feedback that
actually helps me notice and keep up my activity: a sense of
visual progress, some encouragement, or a way to compare against
my own past days or other people.''

\textbf{N3} (maintainer): ``If something breaks or I need to
retrain the model, I don't want to be the only one who can get
this running again -- anyone looking at the repo should be able
to rebuild it.''

\textbf{Objective:} The wearer shall be able to see, in real time,
whether they are sedentary, walking or running, and how many
active minutes they have accumulated in the current session, with
the reported activity agreeing with a labelled reference recording
on at least 85\% of 1-minute intervals during a 30-minute
mixed-activity test. Beyond raw counts, the wearer shall receive
feedback that supports continual self-monitoring.
